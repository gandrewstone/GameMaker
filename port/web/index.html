GameMaker Player

Play games made with GameMaker (1994) in your browser.

Click Play to start. Keyboard and gamepad; Esc backs out of a menu. Scores and settings are kept in this browser.

Games on this page

Your own game files

Drop a game folder or a .zip file here
A GameMaker game is a folder with a .gam file, its pictures, maps and sounds.

Everything runs in your browser. Files you drop are read on your computer and are not uploaded. Scores, saved games and settings are kept in this browser.

Games made with GameMaker 3.0 work. The older shareware games that came with their own player program do not.

It works with a keyboard and, in most browsers, a gamepad. Phones and touch screens are not supported yet.
